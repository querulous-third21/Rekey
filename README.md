## 🎯 What Is Rekey?

Have you ever typed a message, looked at the screen, and realized all your words came out in the wrong language? Maybe you meant to write "hello" but your keyboard was set to Russian, and you got "руддщ" instead. It's frustrating, confusing, and wastes your time.

**Rekey fixes this.** It's a small, smart app that lives in your menu bar and instantly rewrites any text you typed in the wrong keyboard layout. It works across 34 languages, so whether you're switching between English and Ukrainian, German and Polish, or any other combination, Rekey has your back.

Think of it as a safety net for your typing. You make the mistake, Rekey corrects it in a single click.

---

## 💡 Why You Need Rekey

If you work, chat, or write in more than one language, you've probably dealt with this problem. It happens when:

- You forget to switch your keyboard layout before typing
- Your system's default layout changes unexpectedly
- You're using someone else's computer with a different layout
- You copy text from a document that was typed in another language

Without Rekey, you'd have to delete everything, switch layouts, and retype. With Rekey, you just select the garbled text, and it's instantly transformed into the correct language.
